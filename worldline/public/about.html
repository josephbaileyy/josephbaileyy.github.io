Research

Papers & talks

  • Unbinned unfolding for neutrino-nucleus cross sections

    Implementing and evaluating OmniFold, an unbinned ML unfolding framework, on MINERvA neutrino-scattering data. I benchmark it against iterative Bayesian unfolding through closure tests, generator stress tests, bootstrap resampling, and bias-variance analysis, asking whether high-dimensional unbinned unfolding can reduce structural model bias in the few-GeV regime.

    Prof. Benjamin Nachman, Stanford Physics · 2026-present · 2026 Stanford Major Grant

    Paper (PDF) Code

  • Pileup identification for the XCC γγ Higgs factory

    On a four-person team proposing a γγ collider, using Delphes, ROOT, and Unix HPC to simulate and analyze terabytes of collider data and applying ML to study pileup effects on di-Higgs detection.

    SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory · June 2024-January 2025

    Poster (PDF)

  • Gaussian-process bump hunting for the X17 boson

    Used Gaussian-process regression to search for excesses from the hypothetical X17 particle in collider data, and presented the work at the APS Far West Section meeting at UC Santa Cruz.

    Jefferson Lab · 2025

    APS talk (PDF)

  • V-band time-series photometry of AM CVn

    Reduced 140 V-band CCD images and performed AAVSO differential photometry on 138 retained exposures, recovering variability consistent with the known 1051-second positive-superhump family.

    PHYSICS 100: Observational Astrophysics · Spring 2026

    Report (PDF) Slides (PDF)

  • Gaussian beam interference for detecting focal length

    Supported simulation, testing, and preparation of suspension elements, and conducted laser-beam interferometry experiments with Python analysis.

    Caltech LIGO Lab · 2023 · LIGO technical note

    Report (PDF)
